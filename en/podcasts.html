UltraLocal

People with alternative ideas from Obregón

I co-created UltraLocal and I produce it. It's hosted by Pablo Artee, a DJ and cultural manager from Obregón, and sometimes I join him at the mic. It started as ContraLocal; in episode 13, "De Contra a Ultra", we explain why the name changed.

Over 17 episodes, guests have included a Latin Grammy nominated producer, a rapper signed to Warner Chappell, the founder of La Petaka, the director of La Clínica Dance Studio and the first nonbinary person recognized by the Sonora Civil Registry without a court order, among others.

Jutonio

These mothers are from another planet

2 seasons
33 episodes
Born at House of Cosmic
Produced by CosmoMateria

Jutonio is a comedy and lifestyle podcast I created with Donny Valenzuela, aka Mamma Donna, and we've been hosting it since June 2024. We're two aliens from planet Jutonio who came to observe human life with love, curiosity and humor: pop culture, dating, parties, the queer scene and lots of advice nobody asked for.
