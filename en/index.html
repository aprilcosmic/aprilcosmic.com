1/4Who I am

In 2023 I founded House of Cosmic, the pioneering ballroom house in Sonora, because Obregón was missing places to dance without asking for permission. From there I organize balls, workshops and conversations. Everything else comes from the same need: I produce podcasts, DJ, make photos and video, and write. I was born in Tucson and have lived in Ciudad Obregón since I was nine.
